Privacy Policy

Gaura Mala
Applies to version 15 (1.1.0) and later
Effective when version 15 is released

What stays on your watch

Gaura Mala has no account, ads, or analytics. Your bead count, completed rounds, daily goal, settings, and saved Auto Count pace are stored on your watch. The app does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

Optional chant recording

If you choose to record, Gaura Mala uses the microphone to capture one chant, up to 20 seconds, as an Auto Count guide. Pace setup only measures time and does not record audio. The app keeps one saved recording in private app storage on the watch. It is excluded from Android backup and is not uploaded or shared by Gaura Mala. You can play, replace, or delete it in Auto Count settings. Canceling an unfinished take discards it. A saved take remains until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall Gaura Mala. Record only a voice you have permission to record and use.

Daily reset and deletion

Your bead count and completed rounds reset when the date changes on your watch. Settings, saved pace, and a saved chant remain until you change or delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall Gaura Mala. You can reset today's count in Settings and delete a chant in Auto Count settings. App data is excluded from Android backup.

Permissions and sharing

Microphone access is requested only after you choose to record and agree to the in-app disclosure. Vibration access supports haptic feedback. Gaura Mala does not request location, contacts, camera, or health data. If you open the GitHub contact page, GitHub's privacy practices apply to that service.

Children and voice recordings

A voice recording contains the speaker's voice. For a child, a parent or guardian should decide and help with recording and deletion. Gaura Mala does not ask for a child's age or a parent's contact details. The recording remains on the watch until deleted, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Policy updates

If Gaura Mala's privacy practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date before those changes take effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Gaura Mala GitHub issue page. Please do not post private information in a public issue.
