Privacy Policy

Gaura Mala
Effective date: October 15, 2026

Information and storage

Gaura Mala does not collect or send personal information to a server. The app has no account, ads, or analytics. It stores your bead count, completed rounds, daily goal, and app settings locally on your watch so your progress and preferences are available when you reopen the app.

Daily reset and deletion

The daily bead and round count resets when the local date changes. Your preferences remain on the watch until you clear the app's storage or uninstall Gaura Mala. You can also reset today's count in Settings. An optional chant recording remains in the app's private storage until you delete it in Auto Count settings or uninstall the app. Android app backup is disabled for Gaura Mala.

Sharing and permissions

The app does not share locally stored information with the developer or other companies. Vibration access is used for haptic feedback. If you choose the optional voice guide, the app requests microphone access to record one short chant. The recording is stored only in app-private storage on the watch, is not uploaded or shared, is excluded from Android backup, and can be deleted in Auto Count settings. Microphone permission is not needed for pace-based auto counting. Record only a voice you have permission to record and use. The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, or health data.

Children and changes

Gaura Mala does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Gaura Mala GitHub issue page. Please do not post private information in a public issue.
