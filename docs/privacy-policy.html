Privacy Policy

Gaura Mala
Effective date: October 16, 2026

Information and storage

The app has no account, ads, analytics, or developer-operated server. It stores your bead count, completed rounds, daily goal, and app settings locally on your watch. If you choose the voice guide, the app records and stores audio locally as described below; it does not transmit the recording.

Daily reset and deletion

The daily bead and round count resets when the local date changes. Your preferences remain on the watch until you clear the app's storage or uninstall Gaura Mala. You can also reset today's count in Settings. An optional chant recording remains in the app's private storage until you delete it in Auto Count settings or uninstall the app. An unfinished recording is discarded if setup is cancelled. Android app backup is disabled for Gaura Mala.

Sharing and permissions

The app does not share locally stored information with the developer or other companies. Vibration access is used for haptic feedback. If you choose the optional voice guide, the app requests microphone access to record one short chant. The recording stays in private app storage on the watch. It is not uploaded or shared, is excluded from Android backup, and can be deleted in Auto Count settings. A new valid recording replaces the previous one. Microphone permission is not needed for pace-based auto counting. Record only a voice you have permission to record and use. The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, or health data.

Children and changes

Optional chant recordings contain a speaker's voice. They stay in the watch's private app storage until deleted or the app is uninstalled. A parent or guardian should help a child decide whether to record and manage deletion. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Gaura Mala GitHub issue page. Please do not post private information in a public issue.
