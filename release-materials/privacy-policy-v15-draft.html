Privacy Policy

Gaura Mala
Updated October 30, 2026
This policy covers version 14 (1.0.0) and version 15 (1.1.0). Version 15 disclosures below apply when version 15 is released.

Information stored on your watch

Gaura Mala has no account, ads, or analytics. Your bead count, completed rounds, daily goal, and settings are stored on your watch. Version 15 and later also store your saved Auto Count pace there. Gaura Mala does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

Optional chant recording — version 15 and later

Starting with version 15, if you choose to record, Gaura Mala uses the microphone to capture one chant, up to 20 seconds, as an Auto Count guide. Pace setup only measures time and does not record audio. The app keeps one saved recording in private app storage on the watch. It is excluded from Android backup and is not uploaded or shared by Gaura Mala. You can play, replace, or delete it in Auto Count settings. Canceling an unfinished take discards it. A saved take remains until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall Gaura Mala. Record only a voice you have permission to record and use.

Daily reset and deletion

Your bead count and completed rounds reset when the date changes on your watch. Settings remain until you change them, clear app storage, or uninstall Gaura Mala. From version 15, saved Auto Count pace and any saved chant also remain until you change or delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. You can reset today's count in Settings and delete a chant in Auto Count settings. App data is excluded from Android backup.

Permissions and sharing

Version 14 does not request microphone access. Starting with version 15, microphone access is requested only after you choose to record and agree to the in-app disclosure. Vibration access supports haptic feedback. Gaura Mala does not request location, contacts, camera, or health data. The app does not upload or share your locally stored counts, settings, pace, or recording. If you open the GitHub contact page, GitHub's privacy practices apply to that service.

Children and voice recordings

Gaura Mala does not ask for a child's age or a parent's contact details. Counts and settings stay on the watch. From version 15, a voice recording contains the speaker's voice. For a child's recording, a parent or guardian should decide and help with recording and deletion. The recording stays on the watch until deleted, app storage is cleared, or Gaura Mala is uninstalled.

Policy updates

The version 15 recording practices described above take effect when version 15 is released. If Gaura Mala's privacy practices change, we will update this policy and its date before those changes take effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Gaura Mala GitHub issue page. Please do not post private information in a public issue.
